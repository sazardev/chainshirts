# Season 03: Field Notes

Season 03 started with a simple question: what would a uniform look like if it were designed for the city rather than the office?

We started with weight. Heavyweight cotton, brushed fleece and reinforced seams give each piece a presence you notice the moment you put it on. Then we stripped away everything that did not earn its place.

The result is a small, deliberate collection: a boxy tee, a lined zip hoodie and cargo pants with pockets that actually hold something. Wear them together or mix them into what you already own.
