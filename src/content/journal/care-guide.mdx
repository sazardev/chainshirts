# Keep It Looking New

Good clothes last longer when you stop treating them roughly.

Wash graphics inside out on a cold cycle and skip the dryer. Heavy fleece only needs a wash every few wears; air it out instead.

Store knitwear folded rather than hung, and keep sneakers out of direct sunlight. Small habits make a noticeable difference after a season.
