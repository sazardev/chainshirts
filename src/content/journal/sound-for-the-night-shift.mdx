# Sound for the Night Shift

The right sound makes a long session feel shorter.

Closed-back headphones keep the outside world out and give bass a controlled, physical feel. For shared spaces, a small stereo pair with a warm voicing works better than a single speaker.

If you take calls, a dedicated microphone beats a headset mic every time. Your teammates will notice.
