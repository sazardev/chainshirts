# Build a Desk That Works

A good desk is not about spending more. It is about removing the things that get between you and the work.

Start with the keyboard. A 65% or 75% board gives you back desk space without taking away the keys you use. Hot-swap switches mean you can change the feel whenever you want.

Pair it with a lightweight mouse and a stitched desk mat, and the whole setup suddenly feels intentional. Add a headset with a good mic and you are ready for anything.
