# Styling the Graphic Tee

A graphic tee does the talking, so everything around it should stay quiet.

Try an oversized tee over a long-sleeve base layer, wide trousers and a pair of clean sneakers. The volume does the work; the print stays the focus.

For a sharper look, tuck the front into high-waisted trousers and add a structured jacket. Same shirt, completely different energy.
